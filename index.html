Our Philosophy

At Dark Horse, we believe in a hybrid investment approach that balances long-term discipline with active experimentation, ensuring stability for shareholders and an optimal learning experience for students. Our portfolio is constructed using a core-satellite framework:

Core  ·  60–70%

High-quality companies and ETFs designed to provide steady, long-term growth. These holdings reflect broader market exposure and are selected for their durable business models and consistent performance. They serve as our portfolio's long-term anchor.

Satellite  ·  20–30%

A platform for active experimentation and higher risk/reward ideas. Satellite positions are intentionally flexible and may include: emerging companies with long-term upside; deep value or contrarian opportunities; macro/event-driven strategies.

Cash  ·  5–15%

Instills discipline in our capital allocation process and ultimately gives us the flexibility to act quickly when market opportunities arise.

Together, this structure allows us to remain grounded in fundamentals while encouraging independent thinking, curiosity, and real-world decision-making.
